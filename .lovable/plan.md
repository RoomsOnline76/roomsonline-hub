

# Sales Rep Commission Module — Property Acquisition Referrals

## Industry Research Summary

Based on industry benchmarks for SaaS/hospitality platforms:

- **Standard referral fee**: 20–25% of first-year revenue from referred property (industry norm for SaaS channel partners)
- **Ongoing residual**: 5–10% of recurring revenue for the lifetime of the account (or capped at 12–24 months)
- **Tiered acceleration**: Higher rates for reps who exceed targets (e.g., 25% base → 30% after 10 properties/quarter)
- **Clawback period**: If a property churns within 90 days, commission is reversed
- **Payment cycle**: Monthly, with a 30-day hold period for clawback protection

### Proposed RoomsOnline Commission Structure

| Tier | Properties/Quarter | First-Year Rate | Residual Rate | Residual Duration |
|------|-------------------|----------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Base | 1–5 | 20% | 5% | 12 months |
| Accelerated | 6–15 | 25% | 7.5% | 18 months |
| Elite | 16+ | 30% | 10% | 24 months |

Commissions are calculated on the **platform revenue** generated by the referred property (i.e., RoomsOnline's commission/subscription income from that property — not the property's booking revenue).

## What Exists Today

- No lead source or referral tracking on properties
- No sales rep concept in the system
- `billing_global_defaults` table exists with strategy-level default rates — perfect place to add referral commission defaults
- `billing_transactions` ledger exists for logging financial events
- `profiles` table has users; `user_roles` has roles (admin, dev, fearless_leader, owner)

## Changes

### 1. Database Migration (4 new tables + extend billing_global_defaults)

**`sales_reps`** — Sales rep registry linked to profiles
- `id`, `user_id` (references profiles), `rep_code` (unique), `display_name`, `email`, `phone`, `commission_tier` (base/accelerated/elite), `is_active`, `quarterly_target`, `notes`, timestamps

**`property_referrals`** — Which rep brought which property
- `id`, `property_id` (references properties, unique), `rep_id` (references sales_reps), `lead_source` enum (cold_call, referral, event, inbound, partner, social_media, existing_client, other), `lead_notes`, `referral_date`, `status` (pending/qualified/converted/churned), `clawback_until` (referral_date + 90 days), `converted_at`, timestamps

**`rep_commission_entries`** — Individual monthly commission calculations
- `id`, `rep_id`, `property_id`, `referral_id`, `period_start`, `period_end`, `base_revenue` (platform revenue from this property), `commission_type` (first_year/residual), `rate_applied`, `amount`, `status` (pending/approved/paid/clawed_back), `clawback_reason`, timestamps

**`rep_commission_reports`** — Monthly aggregate per rep for fearless leader approval
- `id`, `rep_id`, `period_month` (date), `total_entries`, `total_amount`, `status` (draft/pending_approval/approved/paid), `generated_at`, `approved_by` (references profiles), `approved_at`, `paid_at`, `notes`

**Extend `billing_global_defaults`** — Add columns:
- `referral_first_year_rate` numeric(5,2)
- `referral_residual_rate` numeric(5,2)
- `referral_residual_months` integer
- `referral_clawback_days` integer DEFAULT 90

RLS: Admins/devs/fearless_leader full access. Reps can read their own entries.

### 2. Edge Function: `calculate-rep-commissions`

Monthly calculation triggered on the 28th:
1. For each active `property_referral` with status=converted:
   - Look up platform revenue for that property in the period (from `billing_transactions`)
   - Determine if within first-year or residual window
   - Check rep tier for rate override, else use `billing_global_defaults`
   - Check clawback: if property churned within 90 days, mark as clawed_back
   - Insert `rep_commission_entries` row
2. Aggregate into `rep_commission_reports` per rep
3. Status = `pending_approval`

### 3. Edge Function: `send-commission-report`

Triggered after calculation, sends email to fearless leader with:
- Summary of all reps and amounts due
- Link to admin approval page

### 4. UI: Admin pages

**`/admin/sales-reps`** — Manage sales rep registry
- CRUD for reps, assign tier, set targets
- View rep performance summary

**Property Form extension** — Add "Referral" section (visible to admin/dev/fearless_leader)
- Dropdown: Select sales rep who referred this property
- Lead source dropdown
- Lead notes field
- Referral date (defaults to property created_at)
- Status badge

**`/admin/commission-reports`** — Monthly commission report dashboard
- List of monthly reports by rep
- Drill into line items per property
- Approve/reject individual entries or full report
- Mark as paid
- Export to CSV

### 5. Extend `AdminBillingDefaults`

Add a new card/section for "Referral Commission Defaults":
- First-year rate (%), residual rate (%), residual duration (months), clawback period (days)
- Per-tier overrides displayed as reference

### 6. Cron Job

Schedule `calculate-rep-commissions` to run on the 28th of each month via `pg_cron`.

### 7. Navigation

- Add "Sales Reps" under Administration menu (admin/dev/fearless_leader)
- Add "Commission Reports" under Financials menu (admin/dev/fearless_leader)
